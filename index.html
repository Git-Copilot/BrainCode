Scientific scope: The left cortical surface is an ICBM152 template mesh, not a participant scan. During a command, animated orange-to-yellow waves move across its mesh and brighten with the selected expression value. The atlas has no vertex-level regional mapping, so the moving pattern is a display effect, not measured or localized activity. The expandable body-map schematic summarizes classic human stimulation mapping; its broad regions overlap and vary. Demo values are invented, not gene-expression measurements.
