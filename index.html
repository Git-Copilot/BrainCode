Neuron: fires when the input signal meets or exceeds its threshold.

Learning: attention moves toward each response by the configured learning rate.

Memory: stores the most recent responses up to its capacity.

Decision: triggers when the attention state rises above its decision threshold.

BrainCode is an educational software simulation, not a model of a whole brain, a medical tool, or a way to connect to or control a biological brain.
