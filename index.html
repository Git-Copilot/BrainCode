This deliberately small baseline removes each epoch’s mean, applies a Hamming window, estimates 8–13 Hz power with an FFT, then uses a standardized nearest-centroid classifier. Labeled data is split by epoch into training and held-out test sets.

Research prototype only. It accepts offline CSV; it does not connect to EEG hardware, perform clinical interpretation, or send commands to a person/device. Synthetic accuracy is not evidence of real-world performance. Use properly consented, de-identified data and handle it under your research privacy rules.

Neuron: fires when the input signal meets or exceeds its threshold.

Learning: attention moves toward each response by the configured learning rate.

Memory: stores the most recent responses up to its capacity.

Decision: triggers when the attention state rises above its decision threshold.

BrainCode is an educational research prototype. The browser workbench processes only user-selected offline data locally; it is not medical software, a validated BCI, or a device controller.
